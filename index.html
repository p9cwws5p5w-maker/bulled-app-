How scoring works

Contract-enforced (always true here):
• Mint authority revoked at creation — supply can never be inflated
• Freeze authority revoked at creation — your tokens can't be frozen
• No pre-sale or team allocation — everyone buys on the same curve
Heuristic signals (weighted into the 0–100 score):
• Creator wallet's % of total supply
• Combined holdings of the top 10 wallets
• Whether LP is locked, and for how long
• Sudden large sells by a single wallet
Dev track record: we look up every other coin the creator's wallet has launched and how each one ended — rugged, died, or still alive. A dev who has rugged before adds up to 25 points to a coin's risk score, even if the new coin looks clean on day one.
Alerts: tap "Alert me" on any coin. If its risk jumps or the price dips past your limit, you'll see it in 🔔 Notifications. Tap the bell to choose which alerts you get.

⚖️ Fair launch: coins launched on Bulled open for trading 15 seconds after they're created, so bots can't buy in the same instant. For the first 2 minutes, each wallet can buy at most $100. If the creator buys their own coin, it happens at launch (max 5%) and is shown publicly. We also scan every coin for bundled launches — lots of fresh wallets funded by one person all buying at once — and add it to the risk score.

Volume bulls: each bull fills up with the coin's 24h volume — the coin with the most volume is completely full. Gold bulls are the top 10 by volume. Red bulls are coins with a High or Extreme rug risk score, even if they have lots of volume.

𝕏 Buzz: tracks posts about each coin and flags shills — brand-new accounts, bot-like copy-paste posts, and sudden hype spikes. Real organic buzz is a good sign; coordinated shilling usually comes right before a dump.

Take-profit / stop-loss: set a target on any coin and Bulled sells your position when price hits it.
Momentum / "is it dying" check: separate from rug risk, this tracks whether a coin still has a pulse — 24h volume trend vs. the prior 24h, net holder count trend, and how long since the last real trade. A coin can pass every safety check and still be dead on arrival if nobody's trading it.
Fees: practice trades include a simulated 0.9% fee, so your practice results reflect what trading really costs.
Trade smarter:
• Check the dev's history before buying — most rugs come from repeat ruggers
• Keep buys small next to the coin's liquidity, or you lose money to price impact
• Take your initial out once you're up 2x, and let the rest ride for free
• Set a stop-loss when you buy, not after it's already dumping
• Only trade what you can afford to lose — most memecoins go to zero

These heuristics catch common rug patterns but can't catch everything — a low score reduces risk, it doesn't eliminate it. Always do your own research.

Words you'll see
Memecoin — a crypto coin based on a joke, animal, or trend. Prices move fast, both up and down.
Rug pull — when the creator runs off with the money, leaving the coin worthless. Bulled's job is to warn you before this happens.
Sniper / bot — a program that buys a new coin the instant it launches, then sells to everyone else at a higher price. Fair Launch blocks this.
Fair launch ⚖️ — a launch where bots can't buy first, nobody can grab a huge share early, and the dev's own buy is public.
Rug-locked 🛡️ — a coin launched on Bulled with every protection on: liquidity locked forever, no new coins, no wallet freezing, bots blocked, dev's coins locked for 30 days, no bundles or copycats. Outside coins can't get it.
Graduated 🎓 — a new coin starts on a launchpad like Pump.fun. Once enough people buy in, it "graduates" to a big exchange where it trades like any other coin. See how it works
Bundled launch 🚩 — when one person secretly buys a coin through lots of wallets at once. It's a common setup for a rug pull.
Market cap (MC) — the total value of all of a coin's supply. Smaller = riskier but more room to grow.
Volume — how much is being traded. Shown by how full the bull is. More volume usually means easier to buy and sell.
Take-profit / stop-loss — automatic sells when the price goes up (lock in gains) or down (limit losses) to a level you choose.
USDC — a digital dollar. 1 USDC is always worth about $1. The easiest way to buy here — no need to own any other crypto.
SOL — Solana's own coin. You can pay with it too. Tap the USDC / SOL button at the top to switch how prices are shown.

How a new coin grows up

Every coin in Trenches moves through four stages. Think of it like school.

🆕
New — the coin was just created on a launchpad (like Pump.fun). Its price follows a simple formula called a bonding curve: every buy pushes the price up a little. This is the riskiest stage — most coins never leave it.
🔥
Almost — the coin is 70%+ of the way there. The bar on each card shows how full its bonding curve is. When the bar hits 100%, it's ready to graduate.
⏳
Graduating — the bar is full. For about a minute, trading pauses while the coin's money moves to a big exchange (like PumpSwap or Raydium) and gets locked so the creator can't pull it.
🎓
Graduated — the coin now trades on a real exchange like any other coin. Graduating is a good sign that people are interested, but it doesn't mean the coin is safe. Always check the risk score.
